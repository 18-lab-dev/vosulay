# Getting help

- **Setup and usage questions:** ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/18-lab-dev/vosulay/discussions/categories/q-a). The [README](README.md) covers installing, launching on Linux and Windows, shortcuts, remote profile pages and [troubleshooting](README.md#troubleshooting).
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/18-lab-dev/vosulay/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your vosulay version, operating system, game and backend versions, and logs.
- **A counter works on its own but not in vosulay:** use the [counter compatibility form](https://github.com/18-lab-dev/vosulay/issues/new?template=counter_compatibility.yml).
- **Ideas:** start in [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/18-lab-dev/vosulay/discussions/categories/ideas) or open a [feature request](https://github.com/18-lab-dev/vosulay/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security problems:** report them privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

vosulay is maintained by volunteers; please be patient and follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
